Data Protection

Last updated: March 7, 2026

This Data Protection Statement describes the principles, practices, and obligations governing NyxOps's handling of personal data and sensitive client information in connection with the provision of offensive security services and the operation of the NyxOps website. This Statement supplements our Privacy Policy.

NyxOps processes personal data in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR, and applicable national data protection laws.

1. Data Controller

NyxOps acts as the Data Controller with respect to personal data collected through its website and engagement activities.

NyxOps Data Protection
Email: csectld@proton.me

2. Principles of Data Processing

NyxOps processes personal data in accordance with the following GDPR principles:

Principle 1
Lawfulness & Transparency
Processing occurs on a documented lawful basis and is communicated transparently to data subjects.
Principle 2
Purpose Limitation
Personal data is collected only for specified, explicit, legitimate purposes and not processed beyond those purposes.
Principle 3
Data Minimization
We collect only data that is adequate, relevant, and strictly necessary for the intended purpose.
Principle 4
Accuracy
We take reasonable steps to ensure personal data is accurate and kept up to date; inaccurate data is corrected promptly.
Principle 5
Storage Limitation
Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary or as required by law.
Principle 6
Integrity & Confidentiality
Appropriate technical and organizational measures protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.

3. Data Processed in Engagements

3.1 Client Organizational Data

Network diagrams, system inventories, user account lists, configuration files, and technical documentation provided by clients to facilitate scoping and engagement execution.

3.2 Incidentally Discovered Personal Data

During penetration testing, our operators may incidentally encounter personal data within in-scope systems. NyxOps commits to:

  • Accessing such data only to the minimum extent necessary to demonstrate a vulnerability
  • Not retaining, copying, or exfiltrating personal data beyond what is required for a proof-of-concept
  • Notifying the client promptly of any incidentally discovered data suggesting a pre-existing breach or exposure

3.3 Engagement Artifacts

Pentest reports, findings, screenshots, and logs are treated as strictly confidential and are retained securely for the period specified in the engagement agreement, after which they are securely destroyed.

4. Data Processing Agreements

Where NyxOps processes personal data on behalf of a client as a Data Processor under GDPR Article 28, we are prepared to enter into a Data Processing Agreement ("DPA"). Contact csectld@proton.me to initiate this process prior to engagement commencement.

5. International Data Transfers

NyxOps does not transfer personal data to third countries except where necessary to deliver the requested services and subject to appropriate safeguards under applicable data protection law, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

6. Security Measures

Our technical and organizational security controls include:

  • Encrypted transmission (TLS 1.2 minimum) and encrypted storage for all engagement data
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure deletion procedures for data no longer required
  • Regular internal security assessments and infrastructure hardening
  • Non-disclosure agreements binding all personnel with access to client data

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, NyxOps will notify the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay (where feasible, within 72 hours) and will notify affected clients promptly where there is a high risk to their rights and freedoms. All breaches will be documented in accordance with GDPR Article 33(5).

8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Subject to applicable law, you may exercise the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion where data is no longer necessary or consent is withdrawn
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18): Request restricted processing in certain circumstances
  • Right to portability (Art. 20): Receive personal data in a machine-readable format where applicable
  • Right to object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, submit a written request to csectld@proton.me. We will respond within thirty (30) days and may require identity verification before processing your request.

9. Changes

NyxOps may update this Statement from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above. We encourage periodic review.
