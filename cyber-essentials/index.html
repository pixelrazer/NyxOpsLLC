What is it?

Cyber Essentials is the UK government-backed certification that proves your organisation has the core protections against the most common cyber attacks in place.

The scheme is overseen by the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) and delivered through IASME and its network of certification bodies. It is required for many UK public-sector contracts, is increasingly asked for by customers and insurers, and gives you a clear, practical security baseline to build on.

Two levels of certification

Level one

Cyber Essentials

A verified self-assessment. You answer a set of questions about how your organisation meets the five technical controls, and an independent certification body reviews your answers.

  • ✓Self-assessment questionnaire, signed off by a senior person in your organisation
  • ✓Independently verified by a certification body
  • ✓Valid for 12 months, renewed annually
How we help

Gap assessment, remediation plan, and support completing your submission accurately.

Level two

Cyber Essentials Plus

The same five controls, independently tested. An assessor carries out a hands-on technical audit of a sample of your systems to confirm the controls actually work.

  • ✓Builds on a current Cyber Essentials certificate
  • ✓External and internal vulnerability scanning, device and account checks
  • ✓Tests that malware protection, patching and MFA are working in practice
How we help

Pre-audit technical checks using our penetration testing expertise, so issues are found and fixed before the assessor arrives.

The Controls

Five controls that close the doors attackers use most.

01

Firewalls

Boundary firewalls and internet gateways configured to block unauthorised access, on every device that connects to the internet, including home and remote workers.

02

Secure Configuration

Default passwords changed, unnecessary software and accounts removed, and auto-run disabled, so devices are not left open by default.

03

Security Update Management

Only supported, licensed software in use, with critical and high-risk security updates applied within 14 days of release.

04

User Access Control

Accounts only for people who need them, separate administrator accounts, and multi-factor authentication on cloud services.

05

Malware Protection

Anti-malware software or application allow-listing in place to stop malicious code from running on your devices.

FAQ
Do you issue the certificate? +

Certificates are issued by IASME-licensed certification bodies. NyxOps acts as your advisor: we prepare you, support you through the submission, and help you get ready for the CE Plus audit, so you go into the assessment well prepared.

How long does it take? +

It depends on how many gaps need closing. Many small organisations are ready to submit within a few weeks. After our gap assessment we will give you a realistic timeline.

Are cloud services and remote workers in scope? +

Yes. Cloud services such as Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and devices used by home and remote workers to access organisational data, are generally in scope. We help you define the scope correctly from the start.

How long does certification last? +

Certificates are valid for 12 months. Our ongoing advisory service helps you keep the controls in place in between, so renewal is straightforward.

Why choose an offensive security team as advisors? +

Because we know how attackers get in. We do not just help you tick boxes: we check that your controls actually hold up. When you are ready to go further, our penetration testing services are already on hand.
