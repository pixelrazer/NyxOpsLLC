Responsible Disclosure

Last updated: March 7, 2026

NyxOps is committed to responsible and coordinated disclosure of security vulnerabilities. We recognize the critical role that independent security researchers and the broader security community play in identifying and remedying vulnerabilities that may otherwise go undetected. This Responsible Disclosure Policy ("Policy") describes how NyxOps handles vulnerability reports submitted in good faith.

To report a security vulnerability in NyxOps-operated systems, contact:
Email: csectld@proton.me

1. Scope

This Policy applies to security vulnerabilities in systems operated by NyxOps, including the primary website (nyxops.org) and all subdomains, any client-facing portals or authenticated service platforms, and NyxOps-controlled APIs and web services.

Out of Scope

The following are explicitly out of scope and must not be tested or submitted:

  • Denial-of-Service (DoS or DDoS) attacks of any kind
  • Spam, social engineering, or phishing attacks targeting NyxOps staff
  • Physical security attacks against NyxOps facilities or personnel
  • Vulnerabilities in third-party services or software not controlled by NyxOps
  • Automated scanning generating significant traffic or degrading service availability
  • Theoretical vulnerabilities lacking an exploitation proof-of-concept

2. Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

To qualify for recognition and protection under this Policy, reporters must:

  1. Report promptly. Submit details as soon as practical after discovery, and refrain from further exploitation.
  2. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data. Limit testing to what is strictly necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.
  3. Do not disrupt services. Testing must not degrade, disrupt, or destroy the availability or integrity of NyxOps systems.
  4. Coordinate disclosure. Do not publish vulnerability details until NyxOps has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate.
  5. Act in good faith. Submissions must be made with genuine intent to improve security, not for extortion or unauthorized access.

3. What to Include in Your Report

Please include as much of the following as possible:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
  • Affected URL(s), endpoint(s), parameter(s), or components
  • Step-by-step proof-of-concept or reproduction steps
  • Supporting materials: screenshots, HTTP captures, or video recordings
  • CVSS score or severity rating, if known
  • Suggested remediation or mitigation, if applicable

4. Our Commitments to Reporters

Upon receipt of a good-faith report submitted in accordance with this Policy, NyxOps commits to:

  • Acknowledgment within 3 business days of receipt
  • Initial severity evaluation communicated within 10 business days
  • Remediation within 30 days for critical issues, 60 days for high severity, 90 days for others where technically feasible
  • Notification when the issue is resolved and whether public disclosure may proceed
  • Public credit in our security acknowledgments, with your permission
  • No legal action against researchers who act in good faith and comply with this Policy

5. Safe Harbor

NyxOps considers security research conducted in accordance with this Policy to constitute authorized testing and will not initiate legal action for such activity. If a third party initiates legal action in connection with activities conducted under this Policy and in good faith, we will take steps to make clear that your actions were authorized.

6. No Bug Bounty

NyxOps does not currently operate a paid bug bounty program. While we value good-faith security research, we are not currently in a position to offer financial compensation for reports. This Policy is subject to change, and we will update this page if a formal program is launched.
