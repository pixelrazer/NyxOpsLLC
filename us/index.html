Who we work with

Financial services

FTC Safeguards Rule

Covers non-bank financial institutions such as tax preparers, mortgage brokers, auto dealers that finance or lease, and collection agencies.

  • ✓Unless you run continuous monitoring, an annual penetration test and vulnerability assessments at least every six months (16 CFR 314.4(d)(2))
  • ✓Firms that hold information on fewer than 5,000 consumers are exempt from this testing requirement
  • ✓A written information security program and a qualified individual to oversee it
How we help

An external penetration test plus vulnerability assessments, with a plain-English report you can keep on file.

Defense contractors

DFARS & NIST SP 800-171

DoD contracts that involve Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) carry DFARS 252.204-7012, which requires the security requirements in NIST SP 800-171.

  • ✓A System Security Plan describing how you meet each requirement
  • ✓A current self-assessment score posted in SPRS (DFARS 252.204-7019)
  • ✓CMMC certification assessments are carried out only by authorized C3PAOs. NyxOps is not a C3PAO
How we help

A gap assessment against NIST SP 800-171 with a prioritized fix list, to support your self-assessment and System Security Plan.

Healthcare

HIPAA Security Rule

Dental offices, optometrists, clinics and other covered entities and business associates that handle electronic protected health information.

  • ✓An accurate and thorough security risk analysis (45 CFR 164.308(a)(1))
  • ✓Security measures that reduce the risks found to a reasonable level
  • ✓There is no official HIPAA certification: anyone offering one is selling a marketing badge
How we help

A security risk analysis and an external penetration test, with findings and a prioritized remediation plan.

FAQ
Do you certify us or make us compliant? +

No. We test and assess, and give you the evidence and the fix list. Whether you meet a rule is a judgment for you, your counsel and, where one exists, your official assessor. For CMMC that is an authorized C3PAO; NyxOps is not one.

Do we even need a penetration test? +

It depends on the rule and your situation. Under the FTC Safeguards Rule, for example, firms with information on fewer than 5,000 consumers are exempt from the testing requirement, and continuous monitoring is an alternative. We will tell you honestly on the scoping call if we think you do not need us.

What about CMMC deadlines? +

CMMC timelines have changed more than once. Whatever the current schedule, DFARS 252.204-7012 and the SPRS self-assessment already apply to contracts that involve CUI, and a gap assessment against NIST SP 800-171 is the starting point for both.

Will testing disrupt our business? +

External testing is planned around your hours, limited to the systems you list on the authorization form, and stopped immediately if anything looks like it could affect service. Denial-of-service and social engineering are never included unless you ask for them in writing.

Is this legal advice? +

No. This page is general information about common requirements, not legal advice. Check with your counsel or compliance lead which rules apply to you.
