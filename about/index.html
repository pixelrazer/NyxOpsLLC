About Us

Cyber security advisors with an attacker's mindset.

Mission

We help organisations prepare for certification, stay secure, and understand their true exposure, before adversaries do.

NyxOps brings together cyber security advisors and offensive security specialists. Our advisory work, from Cyber Essentials readiness to ongoing compliance, is shaped by hands-on testing experience. We don't just help you tick boxes: we make sure the controls behind them actually hold up against real attackers.
